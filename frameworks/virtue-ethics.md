# Virtue Ethics and AI

Virtue ethics asks what kind of character, habits, and practical wisdom support a good life. Applied to AI, the important question is not whether software has human virtues, but which qualities developers, institutions, operators, and communities practice through their decisions.

## Relevant Virtues

- **Humility:** acknowledging uncertainty, limits, and the possibility of being wrong
- **Justice:** noticing unequal power and refusing to treat efficiency as fairness
- **Compassion:** responding to vulnerability without manipulation
- **Courage:** delaying, constraining, or stopping a profitable system when evidence of harm appears
- **Honesty:** disclosing limitations, incidents, incentives, and unresolved risks
- **Prudence:** using context and practical judgment rather than applying a metric blindly
- **Temperance:** refusing unnecessary collection, surveillance, automation, and power
- **Responsibility:** repairing harm instead of shifting blame to the model

## Institutional Character

Virtue ethics should not be reduced to asking individual engineers to be good inside a harmful incentive structure. Organizations develop character through repeated practices:

- what behavior they reward;
- whether dissent is protected;
- how they respond to evidence of harm;
- whether affected people have authority;
- whether deadlines and profit override safety;
- whether transparency continues when facts are embarrassing.

## Strengths

Virtue ethics emphasizes moral development, judgment, motivation, and the culture behind formal rules. It helps explain why an organization can satisfy a checklist while still acting irresponsibly.

## Limitations

Virtues can be defined differently across cultures and can remain too vague for enforcement. A claim of good intentions does not protect people from harmful outcomes. Virtue ethics therefore needs rights, measurable safeguards, governance, and redress.

## Application Question

The central question is not "Is this AI virtuous?" It is: **What habits of character and institutional practice are humans developing by building and relying on this system?**
