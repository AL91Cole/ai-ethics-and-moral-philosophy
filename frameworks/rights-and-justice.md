# Rights and Justice in AI

Rights-based ethics protects claims that people possess even when violating them would be efficient or popular. Justice examines how benefits, burdens, opportunities, recognition, and decision-making power are distributed.

## Rights-Based Questions

- Does the system respect dignity, privacy, expression, association, equality, and due process?
- Can people understand and challenge a consequential decision?
- Is consent meaningful, or is participation effectively coerced?
- Does the system interfere with a right more than necessary?
- Is there an effective remedy when a right is violated?

## Dimensions of Justice

### Distributive justice

Who receives the benefits of AI, and who carries the errors, surveillance, labor displacement, environmental cost, and security risk?

### Procedural justice

Were affected people heard? Is the process transparent, consistent, accessible, appealable, and governed by an accountable authority?

### Corrective justice

What correction, restoration, compensation, or institutional change is owed after harm?

### Recognitional justice

Does the system respect people as they understand themselves, or erase identities and experiences that do not fit its categories?

### Structural justice

Does the system challenge or reinforce historical inequalities in policing, employment, education, healthcare, housing, credit, and public benefits?

## Equality and Equity

Treating everyone identically is not always fair when people face different barriers and power. Equity may require accessible formats, additional protection for vulnerable groups, different support, or correction of a historically unequal process.

Equity must not become a hidden stereotype applied to an individual. The purpose, evidence, method, and affected people's perspectives should be explicit.

## Relationship to Other Frameworks

Rights set boundaries that aggregate benefit should not cross casually. Justice evaluates systems and institutions, not only isolated outputs. Care ethics adds context and relationships. Virtue ethics examines character and culture. Bounded moral learning allows pluralism above a protected rights and dignity floor.

An AI system is not just when its average accuracy is high. Justice also requires voice, fair distribution, meaningful challenge, and repair.
